Our Story

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted for Today

Founded by Chef Marco Rossi, Osteria Solaria brings the rustic warmth of Tuscan trattorias into a sleek, contemporary setting. We believe in simplicity, quality, and the joy of sharing a meal with loved ones.

Artisanal Pasta
